# Migration

Most forks diverge and never merge upstream. Treat this boilerplate as a strong
starting point, not an eternal merge target: your app's routes, copy, and
features will drift immediately and should. What remains comparable across
forks is the enforcement skeleton — the gate, ContractSpec shapes, ADR set.

That said, major version bumps can change the skeleton itself (see the semver
policy in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`). When they do, the concrete steps for migrating
a fork from the previous version are listed here — per version, in order.

No migrations are documented yet.
